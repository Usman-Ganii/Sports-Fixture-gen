CSV format guide

Use these exact headers in the first row.

Teams.csvteam,department,sportsExample: CSE,Computer Science,Cricket;Football
Sports.csvsport,format,duration,restFormat: league or knockout. Duration and rest are minutes.
Venues.csvvenue,sports,available_days,start_time,end_timeDays: Monday;Tuesday. Time: 09:00 (24-hour format).

Scheduling rules

Constraints applied by the generator.

  • ✓ A team cannot play two matches at once.
  • ✓ Required rest time is enforced across days.
  • ✓ Venues must support the sport.
  • ✓ Matches must fit venue operating hours.
  • ✓ Venues cannot host overlapping matches.
  • ✓ Valid slots are balanced across teams.
